What this is. A static render that loads the theme's real stylesheets (siwa-tokens → siwa-base → siwa-components → siwa-themes, plus the section stylesheets) and its real behaviour layer, siwa-theme.js. Colour, type, radius, shadow, spacing, focus rings, RTL mirroring and the Arabic font swap are all genuine.

What it is not. The header, announcement bar and footer here are hand-written markup using the sections' real class names — Liquid needs a Shopify runtime to render, and no Siwa store is connected. Treat those three as representative of the CSS, not proof of the Liquid. Merchant settings, blocks and the Customizer are not exercised at all.
