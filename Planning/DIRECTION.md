## 1. Why this set works: five roles, almost no overlap

This is a well-chosen set. Each site solves a different problem, and only one pair overlaps at all.

| Reference | Role in the rebuild | The one thing it contributes |
|---|---|---|
| **Amouage** | **Structure & authority** | A dual-track site: commerce nav *and* a separate `HOUSE OF AMOUAGE` story section |
| **Widian** | **Place → object** | Name etymology as identity; Arabian domes translated into bottle form |
| **Kahina** | **Credibility** | Ethics stated as *numbers and named partners*, not adjectives |
| **Fueguia 1833** | **Narrative architecture** | The story is the navigation: Philosophy → Botanical Exploration → Creation → Galleries |
| **Snif** | **Commerce engine** | The only reference in the set with real conversion and retention mechanics |

Four identity references and one commerce reference. That ratio is roughly right for where this
project is — but it creates two blind spots, covered in §5.

## 2. What to take from each, precisely

### 2.1 Amouage — take the *structure*, not the price register

Verified this session at depth:

- Navigation splits **`PERFUMES` / `COLLECTIONS` / `BODY` / `SUMMER SCENTS` / `DISCOVERY`** for
  commerce, with **`HOUSE OF AMOUAGE`** as a separate section for brand storytelling.
- Collections are narratively named — *The Guidance Collection*, *The Odyssey Collection*, *The
  Secret Garden Collection*. "The Essences" is tagged `the-second-trilogy` — products are
  **serialised**, not just grouped.
- Homepage order: **storytelling banner → collections → bestsellers → store locator → brand
  narrative → newsletter.** Story occupies the first screen, before any product.
- Heritage is anchored to **physical places you can visit**: the Muscat Visitor's Centre and
  **Wadi Dawkah** (the frankincense grove) are linked from the commerce site.

**Take:**
1. **The dual-track nav.** A `HOUSE OF SIWA` section is the direct fix for Siwa's orphaned
   `/pages/our-story` and `/pages/our-comitments`. This is a lighter, more Shopify-native pattern
   than Fueguia's — see §3.
2. **Narrative collection naming.** Siwa's collections are currently `for-him`, `perfumes`,
   `new-drops`. *The Oasis Collection*, *The Salt Collection* cost nothing to rename.
3. **Serialisation.** "Chapter/trilogy" framing turns a 56-SKU catalog into an unfolding story
   and gives new drops a narrative home.
4. **Origin anchored to real geography.** Amouage has Wadi Dawkah. Siwa has **the oasis itself**,
   plus a real retail point in Nasr City — and mentions neither with specificity.

> ⚠️ **Do not take Amouage's product-card pattern.** Verified: Amouage cards show image, name,
> volume and price — **no reviews, no ratings, no social proof.** That is a deliberate choice a
> house selling at **$395** can afford. Siwa's median is ~**$19**, and its single strongest
> competitive asset is **1,212 reviews at 4.98★**. Copying Amouage's editorial restraint here
> would suppress the best thing the brand has. **Keep reviews loud.**

### 2.2 Widian — strong concept, thinner execution than expected

Verified at depth, and this revises the earlier long-list assessment:

- The name is genuinely load-bearing: **وِديان** (*widian*, "valleys"), and bottles are drawn from
  **the domes of Arabian architecture**. Positioning: *"The Elegant Blooming Desert."*
- **But Arabic script appears only minimally on the English site — symbolically, not
  functionally.** There is no bilingual storefront.
- Collections are **Black, Gold, Velvet, Sapphire, Rose Arabia, Limited** — colour and gemstone
  names. Only *Rose Arabia* is geographic.

**Take:** the **etymology-as-identity** move and the **architecture → object** translation. Siwa's
equivalent of Widian's domes is **kershef** — the salt-clay-mud material Shali Fortress is built
from (`BENCHMARK.md Part 2 §2`). A kershef texture language across labels, cards and
section backgrounds is ownable by nobody else.

**Don't take:** Widian as the bilingual Arabic model — it isn't one. **Lattafa remains the
reference for EN/AR execution** (§8 below). And don't take the colour/gemstone
collection naming; it is the generic option, and Amouage's narrative naming is stronger.

### 2.3 Kahina — the most transferable site in the set

This is the one that solves the hardest unsolved problem: *can a brand build on Amazigh culture
credibly?* Verified on the Giving Beauty page:

- **1% of total yearly revenue** to organisations benefiting women in Southern Morocco.
- Impact stated as **countable items**: 100 sheep for weaving · 84 winter boots for village
  children · healthcare access for 25 women · 1 girls' bathroom at a primary school · eye exams
  and glasses.
- Named partners: **Education for All**, **High Atlas Foundation**. Fair Trade certified.
- Labour made legible: *"It takes up to 8 hours to crack argan nuts for one liter of oil."*
- **The women's signatures and identity marks appear on the packaging.**
- Founding story is dated and personal — founder Katharine's 2007 visit.

**Take — four moves, in order of power:**

1. **Signatures on the packaging.** This is the strongest single idea in the whole reference set.
   Siwa's equivalent: a **Siwan embroidery motif** — the Sunburst, tied to Amun-Ra — applied to
   the label of the Originals line, **with the artisan or cooperative credited by name.** It
   converts decoration into attribution.
2. **Quantify the claim.** Siwa's current ethics copy is *"Exquisite Ingredients / Inclusive
   Pricing / Personalized Service"* — three adjectives, zero evidence. Kahina's model is a number,
   a named partner, and a countable outcome.
3. **Make the labour legible.** "8 hours per litre" does more than any luxury adjective. Siwa's
   equivalents sit in the oasis: dates, olives, salt.
4. **Date the founding story.** Kahina has 2007 and a person. Siwa's story page has no year, no
   founder, no name.

### 2.4 Fueguia 1833 — beautiful, and the one to apply carefully

Verified at depth:

- Four-pillar nav: **Philosophy · Botanical Exploration · Creation · Galleries**, each with an
  "Explore" CTA. A fifth item routes to regional shops (International, US, Mexico, Japan,
  Argentina, Uruguay).
- **Commerce is deliberately downstream.** The analysis of the live site: *"editorial experience
  preceding commerce"*, *"contemplative journey over conversion funnels."* Shop links fragment by
  geography rather than consolidating.
- Tagline: *"a proposition for wandering spirits sensitive to the call of reminiscence."*

**Take:** the **four-pillar naming** — it is a far better model than "About Us / Our Story / Our
Comitments (sic)". A Siwa version writes itself: **The Oasis · The Ingredients · The Craft · The
House.**

**Apply carefully:** Fueguia subordinates commerce to narrative. That works for a house selling
rare compositions to collectors. Siwa is a **56-SKU, EGP-only store with a 4.98★ review engine and
10 products out of stock** — it needs to convert. Adopting Fueguia's editorial-first architecture
wholesale would bury the shop.

**The resolution:** take **Amouage's dual-track structure** as the site architecture, and use
**Fueguia's four-pillar naming** inside the story track. You get the poetry without losing the
funnel.

### 2.5 Snif — the only commerce engine, and the only compatible price register

Verified at depth on the bundles collection:

| Mechanic | Snif's execution |
|---|---|
| **Build-your-own** | **Three 10ml travel sizes for $68** |
| **Layering bundles** | $44–$79 — fragrance + body mist(s). *"Milk + Cereal"* = one fragrance + three cereal body mists ($79); *"Fluffy Pancake"* = fragrance + one mist ($44) |
| **Sample sets** | $24 (4 × 2ml) / $30 (6 × 2ml) |
| **Scent bundles** | $99 — 30ml fragrance + 8.5oz candle |
| **Free shipping** | over $75 |
| **Subscription** | recurring discount, member exclusives, early access, pause/cancel |

**Take — and note how well it maps onto assets Siwa already has:**

1. **A three-way BYO at travel size.** Siwa's entry is a 30ml at 375–850 EGP with **no trial
   tier**. A 3 × 10ml discovery bundle is the missing top of funnel for a house whose whole pitch
   is *try this instead of the expensive bottle*.
2. **Layering bundles are already half-built.** Siwa has a Layering line (Apple, Lychee,
   Pistachio, Vanilla), a `layering-30-ml-bundle` SKU, **and body splash + body lotion products** —
   which is exactly Snif's fragrance-plus-mist structure. `layering-vanilla` is the **#1 product
   by reviews (98 @ 5.00★)** and ships with no layering guidance at all.
3. **A tiered sample ladder** ($24 → $30 → $68 → $99 in Snif's terms) turns one bundle SKU into a
   commitment ladder.
4. **The free-shipping threshold as a bundle driver.** Siwa already has one (1,500 EGP) and
   currently does nothing with it.

**Note the register:** Snif is the only reference in the set priced anywhere near Siwa. Amouage
sells at $395; Siwa's median variant is ~$19. When Amouage and Snif disagree on a commerce
decision, **Snif is the one that applies.**

## 4. Where the five conflict — and how to resolve

| Conflict | Resolution |
|---|---|
| **Amouage suppresses social proof; Siwa's asset is social proof** | Take Amouage's structure, reject its review restraint. Non-negotiable |
| **Fueguia subordinates commerce; Siwa must convert** | Fueguia's naming inside Amouage's structure |
| **Amouage/Widian are $200–400 houses; Siwa is ~$19** | Luxury references govern *identity*; Snif governs *commerce*. When they clash, Snif wins |
| **Widian reads as bilingual but isn't** | Use Widian for etymology/architecture only. Lattafa remains the EN/AR execution model |
| **Kahina isn't a fragrance brand** | Its transferable content is ethics and attribution, not merchandising — take only that |

## 5. The two blind spots in this set

> **RESOLVED 2026-08-12 → see `DIRECTION.md Part 2`.** Option (a) was taken: **Oakcha** and
> **Skylar** were added as *tactical* references, firewalled from identity. Note that doc 14
> corrects the assumption below — the note taxonomy and sillage scale live on **Oakcha**, not
> Skylar.

**1. Nothing here addresses the 40 inspired-by SKUs — 71% of the catalog.**
Amouage, Widian, Fueguia and Snif are all *originals* houses. Kahina isn't fragrance at all. With
Dossier excluded and Oakcha not selected, **the majority of Siwa's catalog has no reference.**

Two honest options:
- **(a)** Keep **[Oakcha](https://www.oakcha.com/)** as a narrowly-scoped sixth reference, used
  *only* for the inspired-by line — its price-contrast merchandising and designer-facet filtering
  (`BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3.4`). This pairs naturally with the two-register strategy in
  `BENCHMARK.md Part 2 §5`: lore for the 16 originals, commercial clarity for the 40.
- **(b)** Decide the rebuild deliberately de-emphasises the dupes and leads with the Originals —
  in which case this five-reference set is exactly right, and the decision needs recording.

**This is a positioning decision, not a design one, and it is still open.**

**2. Nothing here is a features reference except Snif.**
The quiz, faceted filtering, note taxonomy and `aggregateRating` work from
`BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3` has no exemplar in this set. Skylar (quiz) and Oakcha (filtering,
sillage scale) remain the references for those, whether or not they join the inspiration board.

## 6. If narrowing to three

Recommended: **Amouage · Kahina · Snif.**

| Keep | Because |
|---|---|
| **Amouage** | Closest cultural adjacency, and the only structural model in the set. Gives the site its skeleton and the brand its authority |
| **Kahina** | Solves the hardest open problem — building on Amazigh culture credibly. Nothing else in any of these documents does. The signatures-on-packaging idea alone justifies its place |
| **Snif** | The only commerce engine, the only compatible price register, and it maps onto assets Siwa already owns (layering line, body products, free-shipping threshold) |

| Drop | Because |
|---|---|
| **Widian** | The two ideas worth taking — etymology and architecture→object — are single concepts that can be captured in a paragraph. Its Arabic execution is thin, and Amouage covers Arabian-luxury identity more deeply |

Both dropped references contribute a keeper — **kershef/etymology** from Widian, **four-pillar
naming** from Fueguia. Record those two ideas in the design system and the reference itself is no
longer needed on the board.

## 9. What not to copy

*(Absorbed from the retired long-list document.)*

1. **Buly's maximalism.** It works because every product has rich imagery and long-form copy.
   Siwa has **1.16 images per product** and five incompatible description formats. Adopting the
   ornament before fixing the content model would produce an empty-looking site.
2. **Vacation's irony.** The discipline is exemplary; the register is the opposite of a heritage
   house. Take the commitment, leave the tone.
3. **Adrère Amellal's treatment of Siwan culture.** It backgrounds Siwan/Berber culture as
   atmosphere rather than lived experience — authenticity commodified. Right vocabulary, wrong
   ethics. **Kahina is the corrective.**
4. **Any reference's multi-currency scaffolding.** Siwa is EGP/EG single-market by design
   (`../reference-analysis/00-OVERVIEW.md §8.7`). Bilingual ≠ multi-currency; build the first, not the second.

## 1. The firewall

The design system already runs a two-palette firewall: **UI Chrome** carries the interface,
**Cultural Accent** carries heritage, and the two must never leak into each other
(`Planning/DesignSystem.md §2`). The reference set now runs the same structure.

| Layer | References | Governs | Must never touch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** | Amouage · Widian · Kahina · Fueguia | Voice, story, palette, typography, naming, architecture, ethics | — |
| **Commerce** | Snif | Bundles, trial ladder, retention, gifting | Brand voice, palette |
| **Tactical** | **Oakcha · Skylar** | Merchandising mechanics, filtering, discovery UX, PDP data modules | **Voice, colour, typography, naming, storytelling, photography** |

**The operative rule:** a tactical reference may determine *what data a module contains and how
it behaves*. It may never determine *how that module sounds or looks*. Oakcha's price-contrast
module is adopted as a **pattern**; its wording and styling come from Siwa's own system.

**The practical test:** if a decision would change a word a customer reads, a colour, a typeface,
or a product name — it is out of scope for Oakcha and Skylar, regardless of how well they do it.

## 2. Correction to the brief: the two sites don't split the way the request assumed

The brief assigned **note taxonomy, sillage/longevity scales and faceted filtering to Skylar.**
Verified against both live sites, **all three of those actually live on Oakcha.** Skylar has none
of them.

| Mechanic | Assumed | Actually |
|---|---|---|
| Faceted filtering (note / family) | Skylar | **Oakcha** — 80+ scent-family values, 28-brand facet. Skylar's collection pages return *"No results from these filters"* |
| Note taxonomy | Skylar | **Oakcha** — Top / Heart / Base with icons. Skylar shows a flat three-word note string |
| Sillage / intensity scale | Skylar | **Oakcha** — a labelled Scent Intensity Scale *and* a separate Sillage Scale (Subtle / Distinct / Powerful) |
| Longevity guidance | Skylar | **Oakcha** — longevity FAQ module on the PDP |
| Mood / occasion filtering | Skylar | **Neither.** No exemplar found on either site |
| Personality quiz | Skylar | **Both** — Skylar's is the stronger architecture (§4) |
| Layering as a quiz *output* | — | **Skylar, uniquely** — and the most valuable thing it contributes |

**This simplifies the plan rather than complicating it.** Oakcha carries more tactical weight than
the brief anticipated; Skylar's scope narrows to the quiz, the funnel it feeds, and its card
vocabulary. Both are still worth adding — Skylar's remaining contribution is genuinely
unavailable elsewhere in the roster.

**Mood/occasion filtering has no reference in the entire corpus.** If it's wanted, it needs
designing from Siwa's own review corpus (`../reference-analysis/06-REVIEWS.md`), not copied.

## 3. Oakcha — deep analysis

### 3.1 What was verified

**Collection page (`/collections/all`)**

- **Sort — 9 options:** Featured · Most relevant · Best selling · Alphabetically A–Z · Alphabetically Z–A · Price low→high · Price high→low · Date old→new · Date new→old
- **Filter group "Scent Family" — 80+ values.** Singles (Amber, Citrus, Floral, Fresh, Fruity, Gourmand, Green, Leather, Musky, Powdery, Spicy, Sweet, Tobacco, Vanilla, Woody) **plus compound values** — *Floral–Amber–Woody*, *Fruity–Gourmand–Sweet*
- **Filter group "Brand" — 28 designer houses, each with a product count:** Tom Ford (14), Kilian (9), MFK (8), Creed (7), plus Prada, Valentino, YSL, Gucci and others
- **Product card:** name · price (often discounted) · star rating + review count · *"Inspired by MFK's Baccarat Rouge 540"* · **"(Retail price: $300)"**

**PDP (`/products/sweven`) — module order as rendered**

1. Product name → gender label ("Unisex") → retail-partner badge
2. Price **$45** · 4.5★ · **10,588 reviews**
3. **`Inspired by: MFK's Baccarat Rouge 540 (Retail price: $300)`** + scent family `Floral-Woody-Amber`
4. **Notes, three tiers with icons** — Top: Jasmine, Saffron, Orange, Marigold · Heart: Ambroxan, Evernyl · Base: Cedarwood, Ambergris, Fir Balsam
5. **Scent Intensity Scale** — this product rated *Powerful*
6. **Sillage Scale** — Subtle / Distinct / Powerful, each with a projection description
7. Specs — *Extrait de Parfum*, 50ml / 1.7oz, **concentration explainer** (30–40% aromatic compounds)
8. Four trust badges — expertly crafted · vegan & cruelty-free · paraben-free · quality atomizer
9. Videos, application guidance, **longevity FAQ**
10. Reviews — rating distribution, **AI-generated summary**, verified-purchase badges

### 3.2 What to take — three scoped areas

**A. Price-contrast merchandising** *(inspired-by SKUs only)*

The pattern is one line carrying three facts: **the house, the fragrance, and the original's
retail price.** Placed immediately under price, above notes.

Siwa implementation:
- New metafields `inspired_by.house` · `inspired_by.fragrance` · `inspired_by.retail_price`
  (already proposed in `BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3.4`)
- `vendor` migrates to `Siwa Fragrances` on all 56 SKUs, ending the schema.org `brand.name` leak
- The module renders on the **40 inspired-by PDPs only** — never on the 16 Originals
- Retail price in EGP, sourced and dated, not estimated

**B. Designer-facet filtering**

Oakcha's Brand facet — 28 houses **with counts** — is the single highest-value structural idea
here. Siwa already has the data: **42 distinct vendor values across 56 products**, currently
producing an accidental designer index at `/collections/vendors?q=`.

Siwa implementation:
- A deliberate `inspired_by.house` facet replacing the accidental vendor index
- **Normalise first.** Siwa's vendor strings mix house and fragrance in one field —
  `Bleu De Chanel L'exclusif`, `Layton PDM`, `Grand Soir MFK`. Oakcha's facet is *house only*.
  Split into house + fragrance or the facet will have ~40 single-item entries and be useless
- Realistic facet after normalisation: Louis Vuitton (4), Kayali (5), Parfums de Marly (3),
  Chanel (2), Giorgio Armani (2), and a long tail — **counts must render, as Oakcha's do**
- **Scoped to the inspired-by collection.** This facet must not appear on Originals browse

**C. Honest framing of what an inspired-by product is**

Oakcha's framing is factual and unhedged: it names the house, states the original's price, and
specifies its own concentration and volume. It does not claim equivalence, and it does not hide
the reference.

Siwa's current state is the opposite failure mode — the claim is *"made everywhere visually and
almost nowhere verbally"* (`../reference-analysis/07-COPY-CONTENT.md §12.5`). One product states it in prose; the
other 39 encode it silently in a data field that Google reads as the brand.

Siwa implementation:
- A short standing statement on every inspired-by PDP — an independent interpretation, not the
  original, not affiliated with the house named
- The concentration explainer is worth copying as a *pattern*: Siwa's own bottles say
  **EXTRAIT DE PARFUM** while its JSON-LD says `Eaux de Parfum` (`../instagram-assets/README.md`).
  That contradiction should be resolved and then stated plainly
- ⚠️ **Making the dupe claim louder is a legal-posture decision, not a UX one.** It is the same
  open question as `../reference-analysis/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md §7.4` and `BENCHMARK.md Part 2 §5`. Oakcha
  operates in the US; Siwa operates in Egypt. **Get counsel before shipping the retail-price
  comparison** — the module can ship without it and still work

### 3.3 Also worth taking, outside the three scoped areas

- **Notes as three iconed tiers.** Siwa's own `coco-woods` Instagram post is already a finished
  version of exactly this component, and is the highest-engagement post in the asset set
- **The sillage scale** — three words, large PDP clarity gain. Siwa's descriptions mention
  longevity on 2 of 56 products
- **Reviews loud** — 10,588 on one product, with distribution and verified badges. Confirms the

## 4. Skylar — deep analysis

### 4.1 What was verified

- **Quiz** at `/pages/quiz`: *"Discover your perfect Skylar scent or custom layering combo."*
  Uses a quiz-score mechanism, classifies into **"Core Scents"**, and has a *"You'll get…"*
  outcome panel. Output is a Core Scent recommendation **and/or a custom layering combo**
- **Collection pages have no working facets** — `/collections/best-sellers` renders
  *"No results from these filters."* Scent families (Gourmand, Fruity, Fresh, Floral) are
  **navigation categories, not filters**
- **Product cards:** image with hover state · name · **exactly three note descriptors** —
  *"Almond Cake, Caramelized Pistachio, Rosewater"* · a **badge** · price
- **Badge vocabulary:** New Drop · Fan Favorite · Leah Loves · Most Loved · Special Offer
- **No intensity, longevity or mood indicators anywhere on cards**
- **"Layering Lounge"** — a dedicated layering content hub
- Scent Club subscription · Skylar Rewards · referral (*"Give $20, Get $20"*)

### 4.2 What to take — four things

**A. The quiz architecture, and specifically its layering output** *(the reason to keep Skylar)*

Skylar's quiz doesn't only return a product — it can return a **combination**. That is the exact
mechanic Siwa needs and has no other reference for:

- `layering-vanilla` is Siwa's **#1 product by review volume (98 @ 5.00★)**
- A full Layering line exists — Apple, Lychee, Pistachio, Vanilla — plus a `layering-30-ml-bundle`
- Body splash and body lotion exist as layering partners
- **There is no layering guidance anywhere on the site**

Siwa implementation: quiz → returns a **primary scent + a suggested layering partner** → both
add to cart together, priced as the existing bundle SKU. This connects the quiz
(`BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3.5`), the layering system (§3.7) and Snif's bundle mechanics
(`DIRECTION.md Part 1 §2.5`) into a single flow.

**B. "Core Scents" as a classification tier**

A small named set of anchor scents that the quiz resolves to, rather than mapping onto the full
catalog. For Siwa this maps cleanly onto the **16 Originals** — the quiz routes to the Originals,
which is also where the brand story lives per `BENCHMARK.md Part 2 §5`.

> ⚠️ **Naming boundary.** Take the *tier structure*. Do **not** take the phrase "Core Scents" —
> naming is identity territory and belongs to the Persona system (Okhtein/Penhaligon's model,
> `DIRECTION.md Part 1 §8`).

**C. Three note descriptors on the product card**

Cheap, high-value, and it fixes a documented problem: Instagram commenters repeatedly ask
*"notes?"*, *"Details plz"*, *"what is the notes for it"* (`../instagram-assets/README.md`). Siwa
already has this data as prose in `body_html` for the Format-C products — *"Citrus, marine,
woody"* (`mawj`), *"Vanilla, Rum, Tobacco"* (`carnal-trail`).

**D. Merchandising badge vocabulary**

A soft social-proof layer distinct from sale/stock badges. Siwa has the data to populate it —
`Best Selling` tag (23 products), review counts, publish dates.

> ⚠️ **Take the mechanic, not the words.** "Fan Favorite" and "Leah Loves" are Skylar's voice.
> Siwa's badge labels are written by whoever owns brand voice, in both languages.

**E. Layering Lounge as a content-hub pattern**

Siwa's `/blogs/news` has **0 articles** and no inbound links. A layering hub is the single
highest-value thing that empty blog could become.

### 4.3 What Skylar does *not* provide

Faceted filtering, note taxonomy, sillage/longevity scales, mood/occasion filtering. Use Oakcha
for the first three. The fourth has no reference — design it or drop it.
