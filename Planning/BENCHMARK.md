## 0. What we are matching against

Siwa is not a generic perfume shop. Three attributes have to be matched simultaneously for a
comparable to be useful:

| Attribute | Siwa's value | Why it constrains the comparable set |
|---|---|---|
| **Business model** | "Inspired-by" house — 40 of 56 SKUs clone a designer fragrance; 16 are originals | Rules out pure niche houses as *model* comparables; they are only *craft* comparables |
| **Price posture** | 350–2,300 EGP (≈ $7–48), median 925 EGP (≈ $19) | Matches the DTC dupe tier ($29–99), not the niche tier ($200–400) |
| **Catalog scale** | 56 products / 158 variants / 19 collections | Matches single-brand DTC, not a 10,000-SKU marketplace |
| **Trust asset** | 1,176 reviews @ 4.98★ (1,212 as of 2026-08-12) | Its single strongest competitive asset — comparables must be judged on how they *deploy* reviews |
| **Market** | Egypt only, EGP, `en` single locale | Regional MENA comparables matter as much as Western ones |

Re-verified live on 2026-08-12: shop-wide review count has moved **1,176 → 1,212** since the
2026-07-27 capture, and the homepage now renders a **Bundles** promo section between "New In" and
"Best Sellers" that was not in the captured section stack (`../reference-analysis/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md §3.3`). Everything
else — announcement bar, 10-link flat nav, hero, USP row, footer — is unchanged.

## 3. The ten highest-value features to import, ranked

Ranked by (impact on Siwa's specific gaps) ÷ (effort on Shopify Prestige 11.1.0).

### 1. `aggregateRating` JSON-LD — *effort: hours*
Nobody in Tier 1 ships without it. Siwa has 1,212 reviews at 4.98★ producing **zero star
snippets** in Google. 54 of 56 products qualify today. Highest ROI action available anywhere in
this project.
**Source pattern:** Oakcha / ALT. — both emit it. **Ref:** `../reference-analysis/03-DATA-SCHEMA.md §8`

### 2. A note & accord metaobject taxonomy — *effort: days, unlocks everything else*
Oakcha filters by 12 note families; Fragrantica's taxonomy is the
industry vocabulary. Siwa has **zero metafields** and 5 incompatible description formats.
Nothing below this line — filtering, quiz, "similar scents", layering — can be built until notes
are structured data instead of prose in `body_html`.
**This is the keystone dependency.** **Ref:** `../reference-analysis/03-DATA-SCHEMA.md §3`

### 3. Faceted collection filtering — *effort: days, depends on #2*
Siwa's nav is 10 flat tag-driven links with **no filtering at all**, and ~35% of the catalog is
unreachable by browsing. Oakcha's facet set is the model to copy wholesale:
gender · note family · collection · **inspired-by designer**.
That last facet is the differentiated one — it turns Siwa's dupe catalog into a browse tree
("show me everything inspired by Louis Vuitton": Siwa has 4 such SKUs).

### 4. `inspired_by` metafield, replacing the `vendor` hack — *effort: days + data migration*
All three Tier 1 twins present the reference as product content with the original's house *and
its retail price*. Oakcha's "Inspired by MFK's Baccarat Rouge 540 — $300" is the sharpest
execution: it makes the value gap the headline. Siwa currently gets the legal exposure of naming
Chanel without the merchandising benefit of contrasting the price.
**Migration:** `vendor` → `Siwa Fragrances` on all 56 SKUs; new metafields `inspired_by.house`,
`inspired_by.fragrance`, `inspired_by.retail_price`. **Ref:** `../reference-analysis/03-DATA-SCHEMA.md §2`

### 5. A scent quiz / finder — *effort: days, depends on #2*
Every Tier 1 and Tier 2 store has one; Siwa has none. Four architectures to choose from:
Oakcha's "Find Your Fragrance", Skylar's quiz→subscription funnel, and
Nose.fr's deeper algorithmic diagnostic. Siwa's own announcement bar already says
*"Explore your Persona"* and 5 products use "Persona" in their copy — **the quiz concept is
already in the brand's voice and simply not built.**

### 6. Discovery set + build-your-own bundle — *effort: days*
The whole Tier 1 set converges here: Oakcha "Build Your Own Bundle
(And Save)", ALT. "3 Fragrances For $99", Snif BYOB. Siwa sells 30ml as its entry size with no
sampling tier at all — for a dupe house whose entire pitch is *try this instead of the €200
bottle*, a 3×5ml discovery set is the missing top-of-funnel product.

### 7. A layering system — *effort: days, highest brand-fit*
`layering-vanilla` is Siwa's **single most-reviewed product (98 @ 5.00★)**, and there is a
Layering line (Apple, Lychee, Pistachio, Vanilla) cloning Kayali's. Kayali and Snif both
ship layering guidance; Siwa ships the products with no instructions. Build a combination
picker: choose base → suggested partners → add both to cart. Lowest-effort revenue feature on
this list.

### 8. Back-in-stock notifications — *effort: hours (app)*
10 products fully out, 49/158 variants out, and the review corpus contains customers writing
*"always sold out"*. Every sold-out PDP is currently a dead end. This is a demand-capture
mechanism, not a nicety. **Ref:** `../reference-analysis/06-REVIEWS.md §6`

### 9. Arabic localization — *effort: days*
Lattafa runs EN/AR bilingual with regional pricing; Siwa is an **Egyptian** brand selling only
into Egypt, in English only. This is the largest untapped audience expansion available without
touching logistics — and it needs RTL support designed in from the start of the rebuild, not
retrofitted.

### 10. Loyalty + referral — *effort: days (app)*
ALT. Reward Program, Snif Society, Skylar Rewards ("Give $20, Get $20"). Siwa's only
retention mechanic is a 50 EGP newsletter popup. With a 4.98★ base and demonstrated repeat
demand, referral is the cheapest growth lever available.

**Also worth taking, below the top ten:** intensity/sillage scale on PDP (Oakcha's
Subtle/Distinct/Powerful — Siwa's descriptions mention longevity on only 2 of 56 products) ·
wishlist (Lattafa) · gift messaging (Snif) · a dedicated reviews landing page (ALT. leads with
"50,000+ verified reviews"; Siwa's 1,212 @ 4.98★ appears only as a homepage carousel) ·
clickable `wa.me` / `mailto:` / `tel:` links · an authenticity page (Lattafa's "Spot a fake" —
directly relevant for a house whose category is full of counterfeits) · a favicon (still none).

## 4. What Siwa already does better than the benchmark set

Not everything is a gap. These are defensible and must survive the rebuild:

1. **Review quality per SKU.** 4.98★ over 1,212 reviews on a 56-product catalog is a higher
   density than Skylar. (Caveat: 804 of 820 attributable reviews are 5★ with none
   below 4★ under `autopublish: false` — see `../reference-analysis/06-REVIEWS.md §4` before promoting this externally.)
2. **Price aggression.** Median 925 EGP (≈$19) undercuts Oakcha ($35–50) and ALT. ($39–69) by
   roughly half in real terms.
3. **A genuine cultural story.** The Siwa Oasis narrative is a positioning asset none of the US
   dupe houses have — and it is currently buried on two orphan pages linked from nothing.
4. **Body-care line adjacency.** Body splash + lotion already exist, which is where Sol de Janeiro
   and Snif built their volume. Siwa has 6 such SKUs on a dedicated template and barely
   merchandises them.

## 1. The core finding: Siwa has four identities and they do not agree

Before comparing anything, the brand has to be described accurately. It currently presents
**four mutually inconsistent identities**, and each one is evidenced by a different artifact in
this repository.

| # | Layer | What it says the brand is | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The website** | A monochrome, all-caps, zero-radius Poppins storefront with no place, no story, no colour and no Arabic | `../reference-analysis/02-DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`; live site 2026-08-12 |
| **2** | **The packaging & Instagram** | A warm, cinematic, **bilingual Arabic/Latin** extrait house with per-fragrance colour worlds and location photography | `../instagram-assets/` — 18 images, verified visually this session |
| **3** | **The name & brand pages** | An Egyptian maison rooted in the Siwa Oasis — "golden sands, shining sun eye and crystal salty lakes.." | `../reference-analysis/07-COPY-CONTENT.md §8.2` |
| **4** | **The catalog** | A designer-dupe house: *Bleu Exclusive, Lady Killer, Boujee Blush, Luna Di Roma, Sundaze* | `../reference-analysis/raw/products.json` — 56 titles |

**Layer 2 is the strongest asset the brand owns and the website reflects none of it.**
The `../instagram-assets/README.md` already reached this conclusion — *"Instagram and the website
are two different brands"* — but it understates one detail. Verified directly from the imagery
this session:

> **The bottle labels carry Arabic script.** `mawj/DOTzj9WFZwB_1.jpg` shows the Mawj label set as
> `SIWA FRAGRANCES` (letterspaced small caps) → **`موج`** (Arabic calligraphy) → `EXTRAIT DE PARFUM`.
> The README records the middle line only as "SERIF". The real lockup is **bilingual**.

That single fact reframes the localization question in `BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3.9`.
Arabic is not an expansion feature to be considered — **it is already the brand's identity on the
physical product, and the website is the only place it has been erased.**

### 1.1 The naming contradiction, quantified

Of the 56 product titles, the number that carry any Arabic, Siwan or Egyptian identity:

| Naming register | Count | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Arabic / Siwan | **2** | **Mawj** (موج, "wave"), **Marasi** (مراسي, "anchorages") |
| Names the brand, not the place | 1 | Siwa Trail |
| Western / English / Italian / French | **53** | Sundaze, Lady Killer, Boujee Blush, Pink Arrogance, Luna Di Roma, Caramel vanigliato, Bare Glow, Drunk Gold |

**A perfume house named after an Amazigh oasis in the Western Desert sells 53 of 56 products
under Western names.** And the two exceptions are instructive:

- **Mawj** is a *Siwa original*, carries Arabic on the label, has the best photograph in the
  asset set, and is the **#2 product by review volume (68 @ 4.99★)**.
- **Marasi** — also Arabic-named — is a *dupe* of 40 Knots Xerjoff.

So the brand has already proven, twice, that Arabic naming sells. It just never generalised it.

## 2. The lore audit — what Siwa Oasis actually offers vs. what the brand uses

The brand's entire lore is currently **one paragraph on an orphan page** (`/pages/our-story`,
linked from nothing — `../reference-analysis/07-COPY-CONTENT.md §465`), and it is generic: *"golden sands, shining sun
eye and crystal salty lakes.."* That describes any desert.

The real Siwa is far more specific and far more ownable. Researched this session:

| Lore asset | What it is | Brand use today |
|---|---|---|
| **Amazigh (Berber) identity** | Siwans are **not ethnically Arab**. They speak **Siwi**, a Berber language spoken nowhere else in Egypt | ❌ none |
| **Kershef architecture** | Buildings made of **salt, clay and mud** quarried from the lakes — insulates against 50 °C heat | ❌ none |
| **Shali Fortress** | 13th-century labyrinthine kershef citadel, the visual symbol of Siwa | ❌ none |
| **Temple of the Oracle** | 6th-century BC oracle of Amun. **Alexander the Great crossed the desert in 331 BC** to ask it whether he was divine | ❌ none |
| **Siwan embroidery** | A **five-colour** traditional palette: palm green, date red, date yellow, desert brown, black | ❌ none |
| **The Sunburst motif** | The signature Siwan embroidery pattern, linked to the sun god **Amun-Ra** | ❌ none |
| **Silver bridal jewelry** | Layered engraved silver (*twet wat*), mother-of-pearl, shells — worn against the evil eye | ❌ none |
| **Salt lakes & springs** | Crystalline salt pools; the material the architecture is made of | ➖ one photo (Mawj on a salt ledge) |

Two things follow.

**First — the five-colour embroidery palette is a documented, culturally-grounded palette that the
brand could own outright.** Compare it to what the repo currently proposes. `../reference-analysis/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md §5`
describes a `DesignSystem.md` built on generic "warm desert gold" `#b18044` and flags that
it benchmarks against **Pinterest**. Palm green / date red / date yellow / desert brown / black is
a *better* palette by every measure — it is specific, defensible, unrepeatable by a competitor, and
it comes with a story. (⚠️ Note, updated 2026-08-12: `DesignSystem.md` **was** absent
when this was written and **has since been created** — it now specifies the two-palette model and
carries the Siwan five-colour set as its Cultural Accent Set. The `skills/` directory remains
empty despite `../reference-analysis/PROJECT-CONTEXT.md §1` documenting seven files in it.)

**Second — the Instagram colour worlds and the embroidery palette agree.** The per-product palettes
extracted in `../instagram-assets/README.md` (Coco Woods cream/wood/warm-brown/gold; Tobacco Vanilla
ochre/rust/amber) are already warm-neutral-plus-one-accent. The Siwan palette doesn't overturn the
art direction — **it explains it and gives it a name.**

## 5. The honest tension nobody has written down

Every Archetype A house — Amouage, Widian, Goldfield & Banks, Fueguia, Ortigia — **makes its own
scents from its own place.** Siwa clones 40 of 56.

Oasis lore draped over a Chanel clone is thin, and it is thin in a way customers and press notice.
*"Siwa: golden sands and crystal salt lakes"* sitting above a product whose Shopify vendor field
reads `Bleu De Chanel L'exclusif` is not a heritage story — it is a heritage veneer.

**But there is a real bridge, and Tier 1 already built it.** Dossier, Oakcha and ALT. all launched
"Originals" lines precisely so the brand story has something honest to attach to
(`BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §1`). **Siwa already has 16 originals** — and the evidence says they
are the brand's true centre:

- **Mawj** — Siwa original, Arabic name, Arabic label, best photograph in the asset set, 68 reviews @ 4.99★
- **Pink Allure** — "Siwa original creation", the cleanest notes data in the catalog, a 2026 flagship
- **Pink Arrogance, Gourmet, Chocolate Creme, Coffee Vanilla, Irresistible Vanilla** — all originals

**The lore belongs to the 16, not the 56.** Give Original Creations the oasis — the Amazigh names,
the kershef textures, the embroidery palette, the landscape photography, the founding story. Let
the 40 inspired-by SKUs stay commercial, functional and honestly merchandised on price. Two
registers, deliberately separated, is a coherent brand. One register smeared across both is what
the site has now, and it is why the story reads as decoration.

Note also: **Pink Allure's `tags` array is empty**, so a 2026 flagship original is unreachable
through any tag-driven nav route (`../instagram-assets/README.md`). The originals aren't just
under-told — they're under-merchandised.

---

## 6. What to do, in order

1. **Decide the two-register split** (§5). Everything else depends on it, and it is a positioning
   decision, not a design one.
2. **Adopt the Siwan five-colour palette** — palm green, date red, date yellow, desert brown,
   black — as the design system's foundation instead of the generic desert gold. It is specific,
   documented, ownable, and it already agrees with the Instagram art direction. *(Bring a Siwan
   cultural consultant in before shipping it — see item 6.)*
3. **Put Arabic back on the website.** The bottles already have it. Bilingual EN/AR with RTL
   designed in from the start, per Lattafa. This is identity restoration, not localization.
4. **Rename the originals.** Mawj proves Arabic/Siwi naming performs. Amazigh and Siwan vocabulary —
   the oasis's own language — is available to a brand named after it and to nobody else.
5. **Make the story navigable.** Fueguia's Philosophy → Exploration → Creation → Galleries is the
   pattern. Siwa's story pages are currently orphans; move them into the primary nav and give them
   the Instagram photography.
6. **Credit the culture.** Goldfield & Banks acknowledges Traditional Custodians; Adrère Amellal
   backgrounds Siwan culture into atmosphere and is weaker for it. Siwans are **Amazigh, speak
   Siwi, and are not ethnically Arab** — a brand trading on their name should say so, name them,
   and ideally source or commission from them. This is both the right call and the defensible one.
7. **Build the Persona system.** "Explore your Persona" is already the most-repeated line on the
   site and it points at nothing. Okhtein's Goddess / Poet / Muse / Heir / Nomad is the model, and
   it doubles as the quiz architecture proposed in `BENCHMARK.md Part 1 §3.5`.
8. **Ship the Coco Woods pyramid as a real component.** `coco-woods/DXH731vCike_1.jpg` is a
   finished fragrance-notes diagram, and the highest-engagement post in the set. The design work
   is already done and sitting in this repo.
