Description

A delicate, yet addictive fragrance that evokes the lingering scent of your lover’s skin. Brought to life by comforting accords of white musk, enhanced by sheer floral nuances of jasmine and glowing orange blossom and fused with a light trail of soft transparent woods, Missing Person is pure, provocative and undeniably familiar.

Notes

Top: Skin Musk, Bergamot Nectar, Sheer Jasmine
Heart: Fresh Cyclamen, Neroli Blossom, Orange Flower
Base: Sandalwood Australia Oil, Blonde Wood, White Musk

Ingredients

Ingredients: Alcohol Denat., Fragrance/Parfum*, Water/Eau/Aqua.

Fragrance Ingredients: 3-(5,5,6-Trimethylbicyclo[2.2.1]hept-2-yl)cyclohexan-1-ol, 5-Cyclopentadecen-1-one, 3-methyl, Benzyl Salicylate, Dimethylcyclohexylethoxy Isobutylpropanoate, Ethylene Brassylate, Hydroxycitronellal, Isobutyl Methyl Tetrahydropyranol, Sandalore, Santalum Spicatum Seed Oil.
