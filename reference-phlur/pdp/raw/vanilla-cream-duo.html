Description

Cream tastes better with a dash of vanilla. Our best-selling body mists combine to create a rich confectionery duet. Sugar crystals, marshmallow, and coconut cream dance on a bed of silky cashmere and salted caramel. The light milky nature of Heavy Cream is emboldened by the kaleidoscopic spice of Vanilla Skin. Embrace edibility with this duo of delicacies.

In This Set

Vanilla Skin - Full Size Body Mist
Familiar and delectable. Hypnotic and decadent. Vanilla Skin, unlike any vanilla fragrance, evokes an iconic creaminess wrapped in a kaleidoscope of spice and intrigue. The dual nature of pink pepper and sugar settle down through rich cashmere before nestling into a core of addictive vanilla and cozy sandalwood. Vanilla Skin is a multidimensional take on an intoxicating sensual gourmand.

Heavy Cream - Full Size Body Mist
A milky impression of decadent sweetness. Playful, sugary yet irresistibly elevated, Heavy Cream is a confectionery delight. A swirl of whipped gourmands comes alive while bright citrus and luminous florals envelop the skin in an evocative, velvety dream. Heavy Cream is sweet, silky yet light as air.
