The business case

The store works. The decision experience does not work hard enough.

Siwa already has a viable Shopify foundation, local pricing, product breadth, reviews, bundles, and seasonal merchandising. The growth constraint is the information layer: product identity, scent discovery, imagery, stock recovery, and buying reassurance are inconsistent.

The data foundation

One product truth should power every selling surface.

The proposed schema is not an IT document for its own sake. It prevents staff from rewriting conflicting product information in cards, pages, search, ads, feeds, recommendations, and support conversations.

01

Product identity

Siwa name, product status, original/inspired relationship, brand, story, lifecycle, and bilingual content.

02

Scent intelligence

Families, accords, top/heart/base notes, sweetness, freshness, warmth, intensity, season, occasion, and climate fit.

03

Sellable variant

Unique SKU, size, format, price, currency, stock, barcode, weight, restock state, and variant-specific media.

04

Proof and safety

Ingredients, allergens, warnings, concentration, claim evidence, rating distribution, verified reviews, and sync dates.

05

Relationships

Samples, credit, bundles, components, layering partners, close alternatives, and complementary products.

06

Channels and governance

SEO, aliases, canonical URL, structured data, owner, completeness score, review date, and publishing status.

→

Commercial result: the same structured facts can power filters, bilingual search, the PDP, Google feeds, recommendations, bundles, review summaries, and future AI experiences—without duplicating content operations.

Confirmed policy guardrails

No opened-bottle returns. No unconfirmed payments. No unapproved prices.

Returns

Full perfume bottles are returnable only when sealed, unopened, unused, and inside the confirmed window. Opened or used bottles are never returnable.

Payments

Display only pay-in-full payment methods explicitly confirmed as operational by the business owner.

Variables

Use [FREE_SHIPPING_THRESHOLD], [DYNAMIC_BUNDLE_DISCOUNT_PCT], [SAMPLE_PRICE], and [LOYALTY_REORDER_DISCOUNT_PCT] until margin and AOV approval.

Consent

Email, SMS, and WhatsApp repurchase reminders are opt-in, preference-controlled, and subject to applicable privacy/direct-marketing rules.

How success is measured

Commercial outcomes, not feature clicks.

Every redesign choice should be judged by conversion and customer quality, with margin, returns, speed, and inventory health as guardrails.

Data readiness

  • 100% unique variant SKUs
  • 100% product type and original/inspired status
  • 100% normalized volume and price
  • Complete scent attributes for active perfumes

Conversion funnel

  • Product view → add to cart
  • Add to cart → checkout
  • Checkout → purchase
  • Segmented by mobile, source, and product status

Discovery and comparison

  • Search and filter conversion
  • Compare usage and second-product selection
  • Compare → purchase conversion
  • Compare → dual-purchase conversion
  • Dynamic-pair AOV and margin

Retention and business health

  • Repurchase reminder open/click/conversion
  • Incremental repurchase lift vs holdout
  • Size-up conversion and gross margin
  • Sealed return/refund rate and reason
  • Core Web Vitals and accessibility

Owner decision

Approve the foundation before approving a full visual redesign.

Begin with the top revenue and traffic products, prove the new decision experience, and then scale the enriched system across the catalog. This reduces redesign risk and creates reusable data for search, filters, recommendations, SEO, feeds, reviews, and future AI experiences.

  1. Approve positioning and vocabulary
  2. Repair catalog identity and pricing
  3. Enrich the top 10 products
  4. Prototype one mobile PDP
  5. Launch tracking and sample pilot
